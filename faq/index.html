Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask us about booking a secure dog field, and about listing one. Can't find your answer? Email hello@wonda.dog and a real person will reply.

For dog owners

What is WONDA?

WONDA is a dedicated mobile app for finding and booking private, securely fenced dog fields by the hour. Dog owners book a slot and pay in the app, and field owners run their field from the same app.

How do I book a field?

Download the WONDA app from Google Play. On an iPhone, until the App Store version is out, you can use WONDA in your browser. Find a field, choose a date, start time and how long you want, then pay by card. Your booking is confirmed as soon as payment goes through.

Is there an iPhone app?

It's on its way to the App Store. Until then, iPhone users can use WONDA in their browser, and you can leave your email on our homepage to hear the moment it's out.

How long is a session?

Each field sets its own session lengths, for example 25 or 55 minutes, and every session starts on the hour or half hour.

How much does it cost?

Each field owner sets a price for each session length they offer. WONDA adds a 50p booking fee, and you see the full total before you pay. Some fields charge a little extra for each additional dog, and that's shown too.

How many dogs can I bring?

Each field sets its own limits, both for a single booking and for the field as a whole. The listing shows them, and the app won't let you book more dogs than a slot has room for.

Will I have the field to myself?

That depends on the field. Some owners take one booking per slot, so you have exclusive use. Others allow a small, fixed number of dogs or bookings together. The listing tells you which before you book.

How do I get into the field?

If the field uses a gate code, we email it to you about 3 hours before your session, so you always get the code that's current on the day (some fields change theirs every week). Your booking confirmation tells you it's on its way. Otherwise the listing explains how to get in.

I'm standing at a field with a WONDA code on the gate. Can I book now?

Yes. Open the app, enter the code, and you'll go straight to that field to book the next free slot.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, from the Bookings tab, up to 15 minutes before your slot starts. The refund goes back to your card automatically and usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

What if the field isn't as described?

Email hello@wonda.dog. If a field is materially different from its listing, for example unsafe fencing that wasn't disclosed, we'll look into it and refund you where that's warranted.

Who can leave reviews?

Only people who booked the field through WONDA and visited it. There are no anonymous ratings. Some fields also show reviews they earned on Google, Facebook or their previous booking system before joining WONDA. These are clearly labelled with where they came from and don't count towards the field's WONDA star rating.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You need to be 18 or over, and your email is used for confirmations, receipts and refunds.

For field owners

How much does it cost to list a field?

Nothing. Founding Partners pay 5% commission on each booking, with no end date, so they keep 95% of their price. The dog owner pays the 50p booking fee on top, and WONDA covers card processing fees for Founding Partners. Fields that join once the Founding Partner places are taken pay 5% plus card processing fees.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe. You connect your bank account once, and your share of every booking is paid out automatically.

What can I control?

Your session lengths and prices, your opening hours, how many dogs or bookings the field takes at once, how many dogs each booking can bring, any charge for extra dogs, and your gate code. All of it can be changed later from your owner dashboard, and you can block out times when you need the field yourself. Change your gate code whenever you like: every reminder sent from then on carries the new one.

I have more than one field. Can I list them together?

Yes. When you apply, add each field (an indoor and an outdoor one, say) with its own session lengths, prices and capacity. Dog owners see one profile for your site and choose which field to book, and your dashboard switches between them. You get an email the moment anyone books, and another if they cancel.

Can I bring my existing reviews across?

Yes. From your owner dashboard, go to Reviews and add the reviews your field already has on Google, Facebook or your old booking system, one at a time or pasted in bulk. They appear on your listing labelled with where they came from and their original date. They don't count towards your WONDA star rating, which only ever comes from people who booked through WONDA, but they show new customers your track record from day one.

How do I apply?

Apply at wonda.expo.app/host/apply. A real person reviews every application before a field goes live. Read more about listing.

Which areas does WONDA cover?

We're starting with Devon and Cornwall. Applications are open across the UK, and we'll be in touch as we expand.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Delete your account any time from Account in the app. To access or correct your data, email hello@wonda.dog. Our Privacy Policy has the detail.
