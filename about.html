About

A safe, private field for every dog — booked by the hour

WONDA is a booking platform for private, securely fenced dog fields. Field owners list their land; dog owners find a field nearby, pick a slot, and pay in a couple of taps.

Who we are

WONDA is built and run by WONDA APP LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17478579) and based in Bideford, North Devon. It was founded by Jay Morrad, a dog owner from North Devon who got tired of the usual compromise: either a public park full of other people's dogs, or a long round of phone calls to find a field with a free slot.

What WONDA does

  • For dog owners — see real photos, fencing height, safety ratings and verified reviews before you book. Choose a time, pay securely through Stripe, and turn up. If the field has a gate code, it arrives in your confirmation email.
  • For field owners — list a fenced field for free and set your own hourly price, opening hours and how many dogs you'll take at once. Bookings and payments are handled for you, and your share is paid straight to your bank after every booking.

Where we are

We're starting close to home, with fields across Devon and Cornwall, and expanding across the UK from there. Every field is checked by a real person before it goes live, and every review comes from someone who actually booked and visited.

Where to find WONDA

WONDA is available now on Google Play, and on the web at wonda.expo.app, where field owners can list a field and dog owners can create an account. The iOS app is coming to the App Store.
