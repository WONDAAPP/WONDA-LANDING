For field owners

List your dog field on WONDA

Already run a secure dog field? WONDA puts it in front of dog owners searching nearby, takes the booking and the payment up front, and pays your share straight to your bank.

What WONDA handles for you

  • Your listing: photos, fencing, safety features and facilities, laid out the way dog owners look for them.
  • Bookings around the clock through the WONDA app, without you answering the phone.
  • Your field in your pocket. Upcoming bookings, what you've earned and your rating, all in the app on your phone.
  • Payment up front by card. A slot is only confirmed once the money has gone through.
  • No double bookings. Your capacity rules are enforced automatically, slot by slot.
  • Gate codes sent for you. If your field uses one, it goes out in every confirmation email.
  • Walk-up bookings. Your field gets a short code to put on the gate, so passers-by can book there and then.
  • Genuine reviews. Only people who booked and visited can leave one.

What stays in your control

  • Your session lengths and the price of each, for example 25 minutes for £6 and 55 minutes for £10
  • Your opening hours, day by day
  • How busy the field gets: a maximum number of dogs at once, or a set number of bookings per slot. Set it to one booking for exclusive use.
  • How many dogs a single booking can bring, and an optional charge for each extra dog
  • Your gate code

Everything can be changed later from your owner dashboard.

What it costs

Listing is free. Founding Partners pay 5% commission on each booking, with no end date, so you keep 95% of your price, and WONDA covers the card processing fees. The dog owner pays a separate 50p booking fee on top. Fields that join once the Founding Partner places are taken pay 5% commission plus card processing fees.

Founding Partners pay 5%, with no end date

Our first 25 fields join as Founding Partners and pay just 5% commission, with no end date, keeping 95% of their price. Every field they refer earns them a month with no commission at all. Against the other dog field booking platforms we’ve compared, that is the lowest rate on the market. Places are limited.

How to join

  1. Apply online. Tell us about your field, its fencing and facilities, and add photos. It takes a few minutes.
  2. We review it. A real person reads every application and gets in touch if anything needs clarifying.
  3. Connect your bank. A short Stripe set-up so your earnings can be paid out.
  4. Go live. Your field appears in the app and starts taking bookings.

Already using another booking system?

If you take bookings through Bookwhen, Acuity, Setmore, Fresha, Calendly or anything else, including a paper diary, you don't have to start again. WONDA switches you over for free, and nothing your customers have booked or paid for is lost.

  • Every existing booking is honoured as already paid. WONDA charges no fee and no commission on anything your customers have already paid for.
  • Unused credit comes too. Block bookings, memberships and prepaid sessions are carried over as credit at your field, used automatically when the customer next books.
  • Your customers hear it from you. One email in your field's name, listing their bookings and credit and linking to the app. Nobody is added to a WONDA mailing list.
  • Your old platform keeps running until the last old booking has passed. Nothing is switched off until you say so.

How it works:

  1. A 15-minute call. We find out what you use today, how many bookings are coming up, whether you run memberships and how customers get through the gate.
  2. We build your listing from your current one. You check it before it goes live.
  3. You download two files from your current platform: upcoming bookings and your customer list. We show you where they are.
  4. We import them. Slots are blocked so nobody is double-booked, and each customer's bookings are waiting for them the moment they sign in with the same email.
  5. Your regulars get one email from you. New bookings come through WONDA from that day.
  6. Cancel the old subscription once the final old booking has passed.

Field owner questions

Does it cost anything to list?

No. There's no sign-up fee and no monthly fee. WONDA only earns when you do: Founding Partners pay 5% commission on each booking, with no end date.

Who pays the 50p booking fee?

The dog owner, on top of your price. It never comes out of your share.

Who pays the card processing fees?

For Founding Partners, WONDA does: your 95% is calculated on your full price, before any card fees. Fields that join once the Founding Partner places are taken pay a standard card processing fee of 1.5% + 20p per booking.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts run through Stripe, which also powers payments for many of the UK's biggest online businesses. You connect your bank account once, and Stripe sends your share automatically. There's no invoicing and nothing to chase.

Can I change my prices, session lengths or hours later?

Yes, any time, from your owner dashboard in the app. Changes apply to new bookings straight away.

Do I need insurance?

WONDA doesn't provide insurance cover for fields. Most dog field owners hold public liability insurance, and we'd recommend talking to your insurer about hiring your field to the public if you haven't already.

I already take bookings through another platform. Can I move to WONDA?

Yes, and we do the moving for you, free. Your listing, upcoming bookings, regular customers and any unused credit are all carried over. Existing bookings are honoured as already paid with no WONDA fee, your customers get one email from your field explaining the change, and your old platform keeps running until the handover is complete. Ask us to switch you.

I'm not in Devon or Cornwall. Can I still apply?

Yes. We're focusing on Devon and Cornwall first, but applications are open across the UK and we'll be in touch as we expand.
