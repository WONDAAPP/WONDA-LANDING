For field owners

List your dog field on WONDA

Already run a secure dog field? WONDA puts it in front of dog owners searching nearby, takes the booking and the payment up front, and pays your share straight to your bank.

What WONDA handles for you

  • Your listing: photos, fencing, safety features and facilities, laid out the way dog owners look for them.
  • Bookings around the clock, in the Android app and the web app, without you answering the phone.
  • Payment up front by card. A slot is only confirmed once the money has gone through.
  • No double bookings. Your capacity rules are enforced automatically, slot by slot.
  • Gate codes sent for you. If your field uses one, it goes out in every confirmation email.
  • Walk-up bookings. Your field gets a short code to put on the gate, so passers-by can book there and then.
  • Genuine reviews. Only people who booked and visited can leave one.

What stays in your control

  • Your session lengths and the price of each, for example 25 minutes for £6 and 55 minutes for £10
  • Your opening hours, day by day
  • How busy the field gets: a maximum number of dogs at once, or a set number of bookings per slot. Set it to one booking for exclusive use.
  • How many dogs a single booking can bring, and an optional charge for each extra dog
  • Your gate code

Everything can be changed later from your owner dashboard.

What it costs

Listing is free. WONDA takes a 10% commission on each booking, so you keep 90% of your price. The dog owner pays a separate 50p booking fee on top, and WONDA covers the card processing fees.

How to join

  1. Apply online. Tell us about your field, its fencing and facilities, and add photos. It takes a few minutes.
  2. We review it. A real person reads every application and gets in touch if anything needs clarifying.
  3. Connect your bank. A short Stripe set-up so your earnings can be paid out.
  4. Go live. Your field appears in the app and starts taking bookings.

Local, and easy to reach

WONDA is run from Bideford in North Devon by its founder, Jay Morrad. If you'd rather talk it through before applying, email hello@wonda.dog and Jay will arrange a call.

Field owner questions

Does it cost anything to list?

No. There's no sign-up fee and no monthly fee. WONDA only earns when you do, through a 10% commission on each booking.

Who pays the 50p booking fee?

The dog owner, on top of your price. It never comes out of your share.

Who pays the card processing fees?

WONDA does. Your 90% is calculated on your full price, before any card fees.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts run through Stripe, which also powers payments for many of the UK's biggest online businesses. You connect your bank account once, and Stripe sends your share automatically. There's no invoicing and nothing to chase.

Can I change my prices, session lengths or hours later?

Yes, any time, from your owner dashboard in the app. Changes apply to new bookings straight away.

Do I need insurance?

WONDA doesn't provide insurance cover for fields. Most dog field owners hold public liability insurance, and we'd recommend talking to your insurer about hiring your field to the public if you haven't already.

I'm not in Devon or Cornwall. Can I still apply?

Yes. We're focusing on Devon and Cornwall first, but applications are open across the UK and we'll be in touch as we expand.

Ready to list your field?

The application takes a few minutes. A real person reviews it, and you can change every setting later from your owner dashboard.
