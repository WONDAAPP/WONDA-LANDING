Already on another booking platform?

We'll switch you over. Free, and nothing gets lost.

Most field owners stay on a system they've outgrown because moving feels like a week of admin. So we do the moving. Your listing, your upcoming bookings, your regular customers and any unused credit all come across, set up by us, checked by you.

  • Every existing booking is honoured as already paid. No WONDA fee or commission on anything your customers have paid for.
  • Your customers hear it from you, not from us. One email in your field's name, listing their bookings and credit. Nobody is added to a mailing list.
  • Your old platform keeps running until the handover is finished. Nothing switches off until you say so.
  • It costs nothing. Switching is free, and so is listing.
  1. A 15-minute call. What you use now, how many bookings are coming up, whether you run memberships.
  2. We build your listing from what you already have. You check it before it goes live.
  3. You download two files from your current platform. We import them. Slots are blocked, nobody is double-booked.
  4. Your regulars get one email from you with the app link and their bookings waiting inside.
  5. Cancel the old subscription once the last old booking has passed. Done.
