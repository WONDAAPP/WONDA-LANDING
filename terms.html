04

Bookings & payment

When you book a field, you're securing a confirmed spot for the date, time, and duration you select, for the number of dogs you tell us you're bringing. A single booking covers at most 2 dogs — bringing more, make a second booking for the rest. Every field also lists a maximum number of dogs it can safely hold at once in total — set by the host, shown on the listing — and a slot stops being bookable once that limit would be reached, whether that's from your booking alone or combined with others sharing the same time. Payment is taken up front through Stripe at the time of booking. We never see or store your full card details — Stripe, a PCI-DSS certified payment processor, handles that entirely.

A booking is only confirmed once payment has actually succeeded — you'll see it appear under Bookings in the app once that's happened, usually within a few seconds.

05

Cancellations & refunds

You can cancel any booking yourself from the Bookings tab, up until 15 minutes before it starts. That cutoff exists to protect field owners from last-minute cancellations they have no time to plan around — once you're inside that window, cancellation is no longer available for that booking.

How you paidWhat happens when you cancel
Card (via Stripe)A real refund is issued to your original payment method automatically, in the same action — you don't need to contact us.
Demo / simulated paymentThe booking is simply removed — no real charge was ever made.

Refunds to a card typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank — this is standard card network timing, not something WONDA controls once the refund is issued.

If a field turns out to be materially different from its listing (for example, unsafe or inaccessible fencing not disclosed at booking), contact us at the email below and we'll look into it — including a refund where it's warranted, even outside the normal cancel flow.
