Why a fenced field makes sense in Devon

Devon is wonderful dog country with a catch: so much of it is working farmland. Sheep and cattle graze right up to the footpaths, ponies roam Dartmoor and Exmoor, and spring brings lambing season. A dog that chases livestock can injure or kill them, and under the Dogs (Protection of Livestock) Act 1953 its owner can be prosecuted. For many dogs, that turns a countryside walk into a lead-only walk.

The coast has its own limits. Many of Devon's best-known beaches restrict dogs through the summer season, the South West Coast Path runs along steep cliffs, and holiday traffic fills the lanes. A secure field gives your dog a proper off-lead run with none of that to think about.

How much does a dog field cost to hire in Devon?

Each field owner sets their own price for each session length. WONDA shows you the full cost, including our 50p booking fee, before you pay, so there are no surprises at the gate.

Can I book a field for today?

Yes. Any open slot can be booked, including later the same day. If you're already at a field with a WONDA code on the gate, type the code into the app to book it on the spot.
